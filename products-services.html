Products & services

From imported technology to field-level guidance, we help hatcheries and fisheries operations access the tools and knowledge they need.

Import — Technology & Aquaculture Medicine

The Eureka Marketing Co. imports specialized machinery, industrial aerators, water-testing technology, and chemical reagents, alongside aquaculture medications — including hormones, immune-support treatments, and Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) — supplied to hatcheries across Bangladesh.

Distribution — Fish Fry

The company distributes fish fry (juvenile fish) across the country, supporting hatcheries and fisheries operations at both commercial and institutional levels nationwide.

Halda River Fish Egg Supply

A long-standing connection to the Halda River, Bangladesh's most renowned natural source of carp fish eggs.
